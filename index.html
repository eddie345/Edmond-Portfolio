About me

A bit about who I am

I’m Edmond, a freelance designer and frontend developer based in Ghana with 2 years of experience shipping digital products for startups, agencies, and scale-ups across Africa and beyond. I thrive at the intersection of great design and clean code.

I believe great interfaces are invisible, they get out of the user’s way. My work is fast, accessible and built to last. When I’m not coding, you’ll find me hiking or hunting for a good espresso.
